About our school

Welcome to Hindatu Islamiyya

Learning the Qur'an and Islamic knowledge with dedication and good character.

A Place to Learn and Grow in Faith

Hindatu Islamiyya is an Islamic learning institution focused on helping students deepen their understanding of the Qur'an and other important Islamic teachings.

Our programmes include Ma'ahad, for Qur'anic studies, and Barna Maj, for the study of Hadith and other Islamic books.

We aim to encourage students to seek beneficial knowledge, practise good manners, and strengthen their connection with Islam.

معهد

Ma'ahad

A programme dedicated to Qur'anic learning, helping students develop their recitation, memorisation, and understanding as appropriate to their level.

  • Qur'an reading and recitation
  • Qur'an memorisation and revision
  • Support towards completing Qur'an studies
  • Building respect for the Book of Allah
📚
برنامج

Barna Maj

A programme focused on studying Hadith and other Islamic books to deepen students' knowledge of Islamic teachings.

  • Study of Hadith
  • Reading and understanding Islamic books
  • Learning Islamic teachings and principles
  • Developing good manners and practice
